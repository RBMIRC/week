24 Hours at Black Mountain College

Monday, December 4, 1944 · Lake Eden, Black Mountain, North Carolina · a day in real time

One page per minute: 1,440 pages from midnight to midnight, cut into 315 sequences. When several things happen at once, the film cuts between them; each class keeps a quarter of its own time on screen, so that four classes held at the same hour can all be seen. Every sequence keeps its sources and its deduced share: ≤ 10% attested by a dated document, 11–40% an attested custom carried over to this day, > 40% assumed, to be confirmed.

Sluglines follow screenplay form (INT./EXT. PLACE — NIGHT, DAWN, DAY, DUSK, from the exact times of dawn, sunrise, sunset, and twilight). “(cont.)” marks a return to a sequence already opened. “(enrolled)” marks students who list the class on their BMC Yearbook record for fall 1944.

Sources: Black Mountain College Community Bulletins and announcements of fall 1944 (Asheville Art Museum, Black Mountain College Collection); Martin Duberman, Black Mountain (1972); David Silver, The Farm at Black Mountain College (2024); Mary Emma Harris interviews; BMC Yearbook (Black Mountain College Museum + Arts Center); weather: NOAA GHCN-Daily and ERA5 reanalysis (Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0); sun and moon calculated. Summaries are ours; short quotations only. Working files, in French: github.com/RBMIRC/24hours · the week-by-week calendar: rbmirc.github.io/week.
